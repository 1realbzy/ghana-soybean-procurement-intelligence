# Interview plan (NOT YET EXECUTED)

## Objectives

1. Test whether information visibility is binding vs capital, quality, tariffs, exports.  
2. Map current procurement workflow and tools (phone, WhatsApp, Excel, brokers).  
3. Identify data-sharing red lines.  
4. Pressure-test willingness to pay (directional only).

## Target respondents

| Segment | Target count (indicative) | Notes |
|---------|---------------------------|-------|
| Crushers / oilseed processors | 5–10 | Incl. large and SME |
| Aggregators / traders | 5–10 | North + transit hubs |
| Feed millers | 2–4 | Demand signal |
| GCX / association / MoFA extension | 2–3 | Infrastructure/policy |

## Core questions (processors)

1. How do you find beans today, step by step?  
2. In the last two seasons, what caused shortfalls: absolute scarcity, late discovery, side-selling, quality rejects, export competition, cash, transport, other?  
3. What information would have changed your purchases if received 30/60/90 days earlier?  
4. What quality specs actually bind?  
5. What data would you never share with a vendor?  
6. What would make a tool trustworthy enough for decisions?

## Ethics

- Informed consent; no deception.  
- No fabrication of completed interviews.  
- Anonymise unless written permission to name.
