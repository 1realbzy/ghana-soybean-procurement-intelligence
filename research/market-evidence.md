## Supported but limited (B)

Micro/SME crush capacity aggregates; solvent vs expeller split; bean export forecasts; north→crush logistics cost magnitudes; Ghana Nuts company claims; GCX last-close JSON snapshot (thin, not a dense price history); MoFA SRID soya prices for **May–Oct 2025 only**; GSS HS chapter-12 trade (oilseeds aggregate, not HS 1201 alone). Prior desk WITS CY2023 HS 2304 ~US$44.42m — **not re-verified** in the 2026-09-23 check (separate year/path from the 2022 Comtrade observation; see [sources.md](sources.md) S4b).

## Conflicting (C)

1. Production 2024: MoFA PBB 2025 **193k MT** vs USDA MY2024/25 forecast **290k MT** — different bases; do not average.
2. MoFA bean self-sufficiency KPI ~100% vs large SBM imports — beans ≠ meal.
3. Local SBM cheaper than imported yet import preference — quality/spec explanation in USDA; not resolved by price alone.
4. Bean exports and meal imports coexist — not a single shortage story.

## Unverified assumptions (D)

Information/visibility is a primary binding constraint. Status quo is only phone/WhatsApp/Excel. Processors would adopt an intelligence product if accurate. Consented microdata will be obtainable.

## Research gaps (E)

Named 2024–26 firm capacities and utilisation; GSS HS 1201/2304 reconciled series (HS-10 API meta incomplete in check); solvent meal share; export-permit enforcement; crushing margins; national soy PHL %; competitor tool survey; primary interviews on constraint ranking; open multi-year Ghana farmgate price panel.

## Do not state as fact in public copy

Processors “lack digital visibility” as proven cause; impact percentages; customers/revenue; any named model integrated; model accuracy; “no competitors”; market demand for the product; that public sources verified to date are sufficient for matching or multi-year forecasting products.
