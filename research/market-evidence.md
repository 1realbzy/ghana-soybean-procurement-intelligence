# Market evidence (soy / crush / meal)

## Scale anchors (not a TAM model)

This project **does not** publish a fabricated Ghana soybean “market size” for software spend. Desk evidence supports the following **physical and trade anchors** only:

| Anchor | Figure | Period | Source |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Domestic bean use | ~198–225k MT | MY2023/24–2024/25 | USDA GH2024-0006 |
| Large crush installed / actual | ~172k / ~72k MT grain | Survey cited 2023 | USDA GH2023-0006 |
| SBM imports | ~60k MT; US$44.42m | MY / CY 2023 | USDA; WITS |
| Bean export value | US$17.66m | 2022 | MoFA PBB 2025 |

**INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE** for: number of processors who would pay for software; willingness-to-pay; addressable procurement-software revenue.
