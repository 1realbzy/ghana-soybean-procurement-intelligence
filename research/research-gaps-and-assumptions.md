# Research gaps and assumptions

## Strongly supported (desk)

- Crush capacity >> recent utilisation (USDA survey figures).  
- Feed-dominated demand (>85%).  
- Material soybean meal imports (USDA volumes; WITS values).  
- Tariff asymmetry grain vs meal (USDA).  
- Northern smallholder production structure (USDA).  
- Thin GCX soy liquidity (FSR summary via press).  
- Quality-driven preference for imported solvent meal (USDA narrative + prices).

## Weak / contested

- Exact 2024 production level (MoFA 193k vs USDA MY forecast 290k).  
- Whether *information* is the binding procurement constraint.  
- National soy PHL percentage.  
- Crush margins and processor financials.  
- Completeness of the “13 processors” list for 2025–2026.  
- Competitive landscape of agri-procurement software in Ghana.

## Hypotheses (not facts)

- H1–H5 in `validation/hypotheses.md`.  
- That a RAG+ML tool will be adopted if accurate.  
- That consented microdata will be obtainable at pilot scale.

## Assumptions baked into the concept

1. Processors will allow historical purchase data into a controlled system.  
2. Aggregators will update lot availability digitally.  
3. Open-weight LLMs can be grounded tightly enough for procurement decisions.  
4. Low-bandwidth UX is solvable without WhatsApp-only workflows.  
5. Policy (tariffs, export permits) remains a background constraint the software cannot remove.

## Required primary validation

- Processor interviews on constraint ranking.  
- Aggregator interviews on data-sharing.  
- Time-motion of status-quo procurement.  
- Pilot data room + offline eval set before any performance claim.
