## Conflicting (C)

1. **Production 2024:** MoFA PBB 2025 reports **193k MT** (calendar-year style official series). USDA GH2024-0006 Post forecast for MY2024/25 is **290k MT**. Different bases; do not average.  
2. **Bean self-sufficiency KPI ~100% (MoFA)** vs **large SBM imports** — beans ≠ meal.  
3. **Local SBM cheaper than imported** yet import preference — USDA points to quality/spec; not resolved by price alone.  
4. **Bean exports and meal imports** coexist — not a single “shortage” story.
