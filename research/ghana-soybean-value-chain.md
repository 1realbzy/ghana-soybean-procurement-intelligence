## 8. Link to maize / poultry

Feed formulas often ~50–55% maize and ~30% soy (AFMOG via press). Maize drought shocks (e.g. 2024) plus meal import dependence jointly raise feed costs and can cut poultry output—reinforcing frozen poultry imports. **ANALYST INTERPRETATION** grounded in USDA/MoFA/AFMOG-linked evidence; not a complete causal proof.
