# Procurement bottlenecks

Chain: requirement → find supply → verify quality → price → finance → move → receive → crush.

| Bottleneck | Desk status | Notes |
|------------|-------------|-------|
| Absolute grain scarcity in catchment | Possible; production series conflict | Need firm-level intake vs catchment |
| Late discovery of available lots | Hypothesis | Core to information thesis |
| Side-selling / export competition | Supported as mechanism class (USDA exports + permits) | Share unknown |
| Quality rejects / spec mismatch | Supported (solvent vs expeller preference) | Plant-level rates unknown |
| Working capital at harvest | Cited in industry notes | Rank vs information unknown |
| Transport north → crush | Structural geography | Cost magnitudes unknown |
| Tariff / meal-import incentive | Strongly supported (USDA) | May dominate software |
| Thin formal price discovery | Strongly supported (GCX volumes) | Informal channels unmapped |
| Information / coordination failure | Hypothesis | Requires interviews |

A visibility tool that ignores quality, export offtake, and capital may miss the binding constraint.
