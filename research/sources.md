## Contradictions preserved

1. **MoFA 2024 production 193k MT** vs **USDA MY2024/25 290k MT forecast** — different year bases; unresolved.  
2. **Bean self-sufficiency 100% (MoFA KPI)** vs **material SBM imports** — different products; not a contradiction if understood, but often misread.  
3. **Local SBM cheaper than imported** yet import preference — quality/spec explanation (USDA), not price alone.
